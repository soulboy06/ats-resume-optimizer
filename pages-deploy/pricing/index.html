Pricing

CheckATS · Updated October 23, 2026

The short answer: The resume checker is free, no account needed. If you want more AI reviews and rewrites than the daily free allowance, buy a credit pack once — credits never expire. One credit pays for one AI review or one AI rewrite.

Free

  • ATS keyword check — unlimited. Paste a resume and a job description, see the simulated score and the missing keywords, as often as you like.
  • AI review: 5 per day. AI rewrite: 2 per day. The allowance resets at 00:00 UTC.
  • PDF export — free. Turn your resume into a clean, ATS-friendly PDF in PDF Studio.

Credit packs

Credits are for signed-in users and are spent only on AI features: 1 credit = 1 AI review or 1 AI rewrite. Every pack is a one-time purchase — there is no subscription, and credits never expire.

Questions

Do credits expire?

No. Credits stay on your account until you spend them.

Can I get a refund?

Unused credits can be refunded within 7 days of purchase. See the refund policy.

Is the free plan enough?

For most people, yes — the keyword check and PDF export are unlimited and free. Credits are for heavy days: tailoring one resume to many job descriptions, or rewriting several versions.

What payment methods are supported?

Checkout runs through Creem, which accepts major credit and debit cards. Other methods depend on your region and what Creem offers at checkout.
