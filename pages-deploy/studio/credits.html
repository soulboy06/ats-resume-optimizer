Template credits / 模板来源

The Classic layout adapts the single-column, serif-heading and ruled-section conventions of sb2nov/resume, by Sourabh Bajaj. Its format is MIT licensed. CheckATS implements the layout in browser-native JavaScript rather than compiling the LaTeX source. The author’s personal resume content is not included.

经典单栏参考并改编自上述 MIT 开源模板的版式，使用浏览器原生排版重新实现，不使用原作者的个人简历内容。
