Template credits / 模板来源

The 30 layouts adapt davidculemann/typst-templates by JobSprout, snapshot e5a1484, under MIT-0. The full upstream sources and adapted layout definitions are retained in this project's studio/typst directory. The adapters support bilingual input, native PDF text, user photos and breakable entries.

30 款模板改编自上述 MIT-0 开源版式，保留原始源码与许可。预览和下载使用同一份浏览器本地生成的 PDF；示例人物及照片不会填入用户的简历。
