Terms of service

CheckATS · Updated October 23, 2026

The short answer: CheckATS is a resume preparation tool. Use it fairly, don't try to break it, and remember that AI suggestions — and the simulated score — are starting points, not hiring decisions.

What the service is

CheckATS checks your resume against a job description with a published, simulated scoring formula, offers optional AI review and rewriting, and exports PDFs. It is an independent preparation tool, not affiliated with any employer or applicant tracking system vendor. The score is not an employer's score and does not predict hiring outcomes.

Fair use

Use the service for its intended purpose. Don't scrape it at scale, don't circumvent rate limits, don't upload other people's personal data, and don't use it for anything unlawful. We may limit or suspend accounts that abuse the service.

Credits

  • Credit packs are one-time purchases — there is no subscription and no recurring charge.
  • Credits never expire and are spent only on AI review and AI rewrite (1 credit each).
  • Credits are tied to your account and are non-transferable.
  • Prices are in USD and are charged through our payment provider at checkout.

Your account

You are responsible for keeping your login credentials safe and for everything done under your account. Tell us right away at support@checkats.online if you suspect unauthorized use.

AI output is a starting point

AI reviews and rewrites can be wrong, incomplete, or mismatched to a role. Always read the output before using it, and never add skills, employers, or achievements you don't actually have. We are not responsible for hiring decisions made with or without this tool.

Changes and contact

We may update these terms as the product evolves; the current version is always on this page. Questions: support@checkats.online.
