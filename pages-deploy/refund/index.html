Refund policy

CheckATS · Updated October 23, 2026

The short answer: Credits are digital goods. Unused credits can be refunded within 7 days of purchase. Credits you've already spent can't be refunded.

Digital goods

Credit packs are delivered instantly to your account when payment succeeds. Because of this, the usual "return the item" logic doesn't apply — the rules below are what we can fairly offer instead.

What you can get back

Unused credits, within 7 days of purchase: if you bought a pack and haven't spent any of it, email us within 7 days for a full refund of that pack.

What you can't

  • Spent credits — once a credit pays for an AI review or rewrite, the AI cost is already incurred and can't be undone.
  • Partially used packs older than 7 days — after 7 days, or once any credit in the pack is spent, the pack is final.
  • The free plan — there's nothing to refund; it's free.

How to request a refund

Email support@checkats.online from the address on your account, with your purchase date and which pack you bought. We reply within 3 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method; timing after that depends on your bank or card provider.
