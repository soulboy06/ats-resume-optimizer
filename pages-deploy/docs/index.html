Frequently asked questions

Is CheckATS free?

Yes. The analyzer, AI review (5/day), AI rewrite (2/day), and PDF Studio are all free with no signup required.

Is my resume data stored?

No. Resume text is processed in your browser. Only AI review/rewrite requests go to our server, and they're not stored. See Scoring and data handling for details.

What does the ATS score mean?

It's a simulation: 60% JD keyword coverage + 40% text parseability. It shows how well your resume matches the JD's wording, not a prediction of hiring outcomes.

Why doesn't the score use synonyms?

Real ATS systems vary widely in how they handle synonyms. We show exact-word matching transparently so you know precisely what's measured. See methodology.

Can I use PDF Studio without the analyzer?

Yes. You can start from a blank template and fill in your details manually, or load an AI rewrite from the analyzer.

Are the PDF templates ATS-safe?

Yes. All 30 templates are single-column, use standard fonts, and export as native text PDFs (not images) so ATS parsers can read them.
