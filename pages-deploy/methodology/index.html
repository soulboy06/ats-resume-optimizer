How CheckATS scores your resume

CheckATS · Updated October 13, 2026

The short answer: CheckATS calculates a simulated score as 60% selected job-description keyword coverage plus 40% extracted-text checks. It runs locally in your browser. Optional AI review is a separate writing assessment. The score is not an employer ATS score or a probability of being hired.

What goes into the score?

Simulated score = round(0.60 × keyword coverage + 0.40 × text-check score)

Keyword coverage is the rounded, frequency-weighted percentage of selected job-description terms found in the resume. A term the job description repeats carries more weight than a one-off mention (each term's weight is capped at 5); the tool does not infer which requirements an employer considers mandatory.

How are job keywords selected and matched?

The browser loads a cross-occupation skills dictionary, then selects up to 40 dictionary matches from the job description. If fewer than five are found, it supplements them with statistical word extraction, excluding common filler words. If the dictionary cannot load, this fallback can change the selected terms.

Generic soft-skill buzzwords are excluded from the keyword list in both paths: terms like communicate, detail-oriented, team player, or time management appear in nearly every resume, so counting them would inflate coverage without signal. The score therefore reflects hard-skill and tool coverage rather than how many filler phrases a resume contains.

Resume matching ignores case, normalizes whitespace and long dashes, and checks Latin word boundaries. Chinese terms use direct substring matching. It does not expand synonyms, stem words, or map acronyms: K8s alone does not match Kubernetes, and Amazon Web Services alone does not match AWS in this tool. Repeating an already matched term does not raise coverage.

The four extracted-text checks

Each passing check adds 25 points to the text-check score, which contributes up to 40 points to the overall score.

CheckCurrent rule
ContactAn email address is found. Phone detection is supplementary and does not determine whether this check passes.
DatesAt least two supported month dates, all in the same format: YYYY.MM, YYYY-MM, MM/YYYY, or Chinese year/month dates. English month names such as Jan 2024 are not recognized by this check.
Clean textNo replacement characters, at most two private-use characters, and no more than 5% other symbol characters after whitespace is removed.
LengthBetween 300 and 3,000 non-whitespace Unicode characters. This heuristic does not determine an ideal page count or resume length for every role.

A worked example

If the selected job terms are Python (mentioned 4 times), SQL (3 times), Docker, Kubernetes, and AWS (once each), and the resume contains Python, Docker, Kubernetes, and AWS but not SQL, the weighted coverage is (4+1+1+1) ÷ (4+3+1+1+1) = 70%. Passing all four text checks gives 100%. The simulated score is round(0.60 × 70 + 0.40 × 100) = 82/100. These are illustrative inputs, not an employer benchmark.

What this score cannot measure

  • Original PDF layout, columns, tables, section detection, or how an employer's parser reads them.
  • Scanned or image-only PDFs: CheckATS has no OCR. It accepts pasted text or text-based PDF imports, not DOCX uploads.
  • Employer screening questions, work authorization, hiring policies, or interview decisions.
  • Experience quality or verified accomplishments: these require human review. AI suggestions can be mistaken.

For unchanged inputs and the same selected terms, the local score is deterministic. AI reviews and rewrites may vary and should be checked before use. Include missing terms only when they truthfully describe your experience.

PDF Resume Studio

Homepage PDF upload extracts text for analysis without displaying a page preview. After AI Rewrite, Save as PDF opens the separate studio and carries the rewritten text within the same browser tab. Local rules identify sections; review the name, contact information and every section. Unrecognized content is retained for editing.

The studio offers three single-column templates, an optional photo, paper sizes and text-size controls. It creates a new template layout rather than preserving the uploaded PDF. Preview and download use the same native-text PDF, with automatic pagination, no watermark, and a text-extraction check before download. Processing remains local; photos are not sent to AI. Drafts use this tab's browser session storage. No template guarantees compatibility with every employer's parser; check every page before using it.

How is this different from Greenhouse or Workday?

CheckATS is an independent preparation tool and is not affiliated with those vendors. ATS features and employer configurations vary. For example, Greenhouse's Talent Matching FAQ describes related-term matching and says that feature does not automatically reject or advance candidates. CheckATS does not reproduce that system or Workday's screening.

Is it free?

Local checks require no account and do not use the AI allowance. Standard AI limits are 5 review requests and 2 rewrite requests per IP per UTC day, resetting at 00:00 UTC. A shared network shares its IP allowance. Automatic review refreshes after a rewrite also consume review requests, and a failed request can consume an allowance.

Where is my resume processed?

  • Local checks and PDF extraction: run in this browser tab; the scoring code does not upload the resume for these operations.
  • AI review: sends the resume and job description to the site's Cloudflare Worker, which uses Cloudflare Workers AI. The current review request uses up to 6,000 resume characters and up to 800 job-description characters.
  • AI rewrite: sends the resume, job description, and missing terms to the Worker and DeepSeek. Review the returned text and highlighted changes; the prompt's instructions cannot guarantee factual accuracy.
  • Email delivery: sends the recipient address and optimized resume to Resend when requested.
  • Feedback: submissions may be stored, including contact details and IP address. Avoid including resume data in feedback unless you intend to submit it.

The site's Worker uses IP-based request counters. Provider logging and retention are governed by their policies; local scoring does not establish a guarantee about external AI or email retention. The site also loads third-party fonts and analytics; PDF libraries are self-hosted.
