Privacy policy

CheckATS · Updated October 23, 2026

The short answer: Your resume is processed in your browser. We only receive it when you explicitly click an AI feature. If you sign in, we store your email and your credit balance — nothing else. We never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Account: if you sign in, we store your email address and your credit balance.
  • AI requests: when you click AI review or AI rewrite, your resume text and the job description are sent to our Cloudflare Worker so the AI can process them.
  • Feedback: whatever you type into the feedback form, which may include contact details.
  • Anonymous analytics: Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views. No cross-site tracking, no advertising profiles.

What never leaves your browser

The ATS keyword check, the skills-dictionary matching, and PDF text extraction all run locally in your browser tab. Your resume is not uploaded for any of these. PDF Studio drafts live in your browser's session storage and disappear when the tab closes.

Who else sees your data

AI review requests go to Cloudflare Workers AI; AI rewrites also go to DeepSeek. Resume emails you choose to send go through Resend. That is the whole list — we do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

How long we keep it

Account records (email, credits) are kept while your account exists. AI request logs are kept briefly for rate limiting and abuse prevention, then discarded. You can ask us to delete your account data at any time.

Your rights

Ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it: support@checkats.online. We reply within a few business days.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@checkats.online.
