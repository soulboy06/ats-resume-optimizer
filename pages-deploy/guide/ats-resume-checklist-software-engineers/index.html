ATS Resume Checklist for Software Engineers (2026)

CheckATS · Updated October 13, 2026

Use this 10-point checklist to make your experience easy to read and relevant to a software engineering role. ATS products and employer settings vary; no checklist guarantees an interview or a passing score.

The short answer: use the job description's exact words, keep a single-column text-based layout with standard section headers, put a dedicated Skills section near the top, quantify your impact with numbers, and submit as a selectable-text PDF or DOCX. These are practical writing and formatting recommendations, not a universal ATS scoring formula.

What actually gets a software engineer's resume past an ATS?

  1. Mirror the job description's exact words

    Use the job description's terminology when it accurately describes your experience. CheckATS uses case-insensitive exact matching: "K8s" does not match "Kubernetes" in this tool. Other systems can recognize related terms; Greenhouse Talent Matching explicitly supports that. Include both an acronym and its full term where useful, without claiming skills you do not have.

  2. Keep the layout to a single column of plain text

    A single-column layout is a conservative starting point. Extraction results vary by parser, so check the extracted text and reading order instead of assuming every multi-column resume fails. CheckATS evaluates extracted text, not the original layout.

  3. Use standard section headers

    "Experience," "Skills," "Education." Familiar labels help readers locate information quickly. Whether a parser identifies a section depends on the product; CheckATS does not test section detection.

  4. Put a dedicated Skills section near the top

    List exact technology names — languages, frameworks, cloud platforms, tools — spelled exactly as they appear in job posts. This is the highest-density keyword area of your resume.

  5. Quantify everything a hiring manager would care about

    "Reduced p99 latency by 40%," "led migration serving 2M daily users," "cut deploy time from 2 hours to 15 minutes." Use only figures you can substantiate. This is advice for human readers and the separate AI review, not a component of the local ATS score.

  6. Keep dates in one consistent format

    Use one clear month-and-year style consistently. CheckATS currently recognizes formats such as "2021-01", "2021.01", "01/2021", and Chinese year/month dates; its mechanical date check does not recognize "Jan 2021". That limitation does not mean other ATS products cannot read it.

  7. Make contact info machine-readable

    Email and phone on their own lines in plain text — not inside a header image, icon, or graphic. Check that your email survives extraction. CheckATS checks for an email address; a telephone number is optional for that check.

  8. Submit as a text-based PDF or DOCX

    Follow the employer's requested file type. CheckATS imports text-based PDFs and pasted text; it does not import DOCX or perform OCR on scanned PDFs. Selecting and copying the text is a useful first check, but other systems may have OCR support.

  9. Tailor one resume per application

    Tailoring your resume can make relevant experience easier to find, although it cannot guarantee an interview. Swap the skills emphasis and reword 2–3 bullets per JD — small effort, large difference in keyword coverage.

  10. Run an ATS check before you submit

    Paste your resume and the JD into a checker and confirm the must-have keywords actually appear. Thirty seconds of verification beats wondering why you never heard back.

Frequently asked questions

Do recruiters really use ATS to auto-reject resumes?

It depends on the product and employer settings. Greenhouse states that its Talent Matching feature does not auto-reject or auto-advance candidates. CheckATS does not access employer screening rules and cannot predict a hiring decision; do not interpret its score as a rejection probability.

Should software engineers use a fancy resume template?

A clean, single-column version is a conservative option for online applications. Follow the employer's instructions and check the extracted text; a designed template does not automatically fail every ATS.

How many JD keywords are enough?

Cover the must-haves first. Chasing 100% coverage on nice-to-haves has diminishing returns — and stuffing keywords where they don't belong reads badly to the human who eventually reviews your resume.

What is the single fastest ATS win for a software engineer?

Mirror the job description's exact wording in a dedicated Skills section near the top of a single-column, text-based resume. Use only accurate skills, and verify that the extracted text remains readable. These steps improve clarity but do not guarantee selection.
